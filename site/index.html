The TGA Rebuild Problem

The Treasury General Account (TGA) is the U.S. government's checking account at the Federal Reserve. When Treasury issues new securities—particularly short-term bills—the proceeds flow into the TGA, draining liquidity from the financial system. These "rebuild" episodes can move hundreds of billions of dollars in weeks, yet the transmission channels are poorly understood.

This project asks: which aggregate balance-sheet stocks move around rebuilds? Drag to zoom, hover for values, double-click to reset.

This interactive display retains an exploratory specification. Its numerical results are separate from the canonical pre-December-2025 estimates described in the repository README. Monthly MMF data are carried into a weekly panel; the charts do not observe weekly MMF transactions.

1. MMF Treasury Holdings Rise Around Bill-Size Innovations

Using a bill offering-size innovation (see Methods), we find that a one-standard-deviation ($--B) increase in the bill-size innovation is associated with MMF Treasury holdings to rise by $--B over 4 weeks (t=--, Newey-West).

Interpretation: MMF Treasury holdings rise and ON RRP declines around larger bill-size innovations. These overlapping channel coefficients do not identify funding shares or household behavior. Statistically insignificant bank outcomes do not establish an absence of bank involvement.

Methods

Data Pipeline

Weekly panel: -- observations, -- variables, ----–----. All values in millions of USD. TGA from DTS Wednesday closing balances (not FRED WTREGEN weekly average). H.8 series converted from billions. OFR monthly data forward-filled via merge_asof.

Event Detection

Flagged when weekly ΔTGA or rolling 4w/8w change exceeds the 90th percentile of positive changes only. Contiguous flagged weeks (max 9-day gap) grouped into events.

Bill Offering-Size Deviation

surprise = offering_amt − trailing_median(last 8 same-term × reopening-status auctions, min 4). Summed to weekly. Tax-receipt surprise (DTS deposits) as control. A same-term-only grouping is tracked separately as a robustness comparison. This trailing-median deviation does not measure market expectations or constitute an instrument.

Local Projections

Δyt+h = α + β(h) · shockt + γ · controlst + εt+h

Baseline (binary shock): 2 shock lags, 1 response lag, 11 month dummies. Bill-surprise specification adds a tax-receipt surprise control. Newey-West HAC SEs (bandwidth = horizon). Placebo tests at h=−4 to h=−1.

Caveats: (1) Describes associations with bill offering-size deviations. (2) h=−1 ON RRP pre-trend from same-week announcement. (3) Persistence limits interpretation as a single isolated event. (4) CMB comparisons also have pre-trends and do not establish a mechanism.

Limitations

  • Issuance-specific. Does not capture tax receipts, coupon issuance, or spending.
  • Weekly frequency. May miss intra-week dynamics.
  • Shock persistence. Autocorrelation = 0.86. Long-horizon effects contaminated.
  • NSA baseline. Month dummies control seasonality. Seasonally adjusted series provide a sensitivity comparison.
  • Accounting overlap. Channels are not mutually exclusive.
  • Separate allocation extension. SEC N-MFP event-window comparisons neither validate the canonical estimator nor independently establish its mechanism. Exact extension results require a separate receipt.
